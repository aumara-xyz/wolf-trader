WOLF PAPER

About

WOLF is a paper desk on this computer. It reads public Polymarket prices and writes simulated fills into a local book. There is no wallet on this page, and nothing here is sent to an exchange.

Modes

  • PAPER is the default. Cash is simulated. Prices are public. Spend is off.
  • LIVE_TAPE may read keyless public prices. The money stays paper.
  • LIVE_MONEY is refused. A real order needs an AUKORA-37 grant. That grant is not in this repo, and this desk will not invent one.

Book

Each fill keeps a receipt: market, price, source, time. Audit replays those receipts against cash and positions. Kill stops new fills. A loss cap stops them if equity falls through the session floor.

This is not investment advice. A paper fill is not a prediction and not a promise.
